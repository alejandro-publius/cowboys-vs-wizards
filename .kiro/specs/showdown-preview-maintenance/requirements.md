# Showdown preview maintenance

- **PM1.** When a player stops the troop reel during an attack, the gallery shall clear the active replay and return to the selected card model.
- **PM2.** When a player returns from a local match, Camp shall restore that room and control. When the player later selects Play from the title, Camp shall open on the Trail without reopening the previous room.
- **PM3.** When a declared check fails, the preview workflow shall give that repair to its assigned Kiro agent in its own checkout. The agent shall change only its assigned source file.
- **PM4.** When independent repairs have been combined, the preview workflow shall rebuild and run the complete gate before changing the playable preview. A failing build shall leave the last accepted preview in place.
