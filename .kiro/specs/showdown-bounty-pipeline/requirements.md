# Requirements: Showdown Wanted Bounty Draft & Agent Ownership Pipeline

## Normative requirements

- SBP-1: WHEN `scripts/maintain-showdown-preview.mjs` loads `.kiro/specs/showdown-preview-maintenance/repairs.md` or `repairs.json`, THE runner SHALL verify each repair case's `agent` against its pinned single-file ownership map (`showdown-preview-maintainer` -> `src/ui/showdown-3d/gallery.js`, `showdown-camp-maintainer` -> `src/app.js`) and reject any mismatched or shell-enabled writer profile.
- SBP-2: THE `showdown-bounty-drafter` custom agent SHALL allow `fs_write` exclusively under `tasks/showdown-bounties/**`, carry no `shell` tool, and share zero `fs_write` allow paths with any other protected writer agent in `.kiro/agents/`.
- SBP-3: THE `showdown-bounty-reviewer` custom agent SHALL deny all `fs_write` operations (`**`) and allow `shell` execution only for `node scripts/verify-showdown-bounty-draft.mjs prepare` and `node scripts/verify-showdown-bounty-draft.mjs verify`.
- SBP-4: WHEN `node scripts/verify-showdown-bounty-draft.mjs verify <dir>` inspects a draft package, THE verifier SHALL require `bounty.json`, `reward.md`, `portrait-brief.md`, and `model-brief.md`, rejecting missing files, non-unique bounty IDs or names against `BOUNTIES`, unknown Wizard boss or Cowboy reward types in `TYPES`, or missing human-art approval gates in the briefs.
- SBP-5: THE verifier SHALL enforce Showdown escort-deck legality on `bounty.json`: a 16-card Wizard-only deck (`DECK_SIZE === 16`), positive integer copy counts within each card's `RARITY` copy cap, and a valid entrance mode (`"board"` without `lands` or `"lands"` with an integer `lands` between `2` and `8`).
- SBP-6: THE verifier SHALL require exactly `HUNTS` (`5`) progressive hunt stages with valid bot levels (`0`, `1`, `1.5`, or `2`), Base orbs in `3..6`, positive integer boss orbs (`1..12`), and monotonically non-decreasing `level` and `orbs` from hunt `1` through hunt `5`.
- SBP-7: WHEN all structural checks pass, THE verifier SHALL simulate every hunt stage against the post-Trail Cowboy deck (`deckAfter(TRAIL.length)`) across a deterministic seed cohort (`seeds 0..23`) using the pure Showdown engine (`createShowdown` and `playTurn`), requiring at least one deterministic Cowboy victory and zero engine errors on every hunt stage while reporting empirical win rates.
- SBP-8: WHEN `.kiro/agents/` and tracked workflows/scripts are audited by `scripts/scorecard.mjs`, THE `agents` row SHALL measure at least `8` active protected writer agents with zero unprotected writers and zero shared write paths.
