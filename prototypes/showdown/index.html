Showdown prototype

    All the rules
    1. You choose every attack: pick your troop, then the enemy troop it hits.
    2. Damage beyond a troop's last orb hits its owner's Base. A troop that survives hits back.
    3. A Base can be struck directly only when its side has no troops.
    4. Play first, then attack: one troop, one spell and one trap a turn. A free troop rests on the turn it arrives; a tribute troop strikes at once.
    5. A trap waits face down and springs on the next enemy attack.
    6. An empty deck costs your Base an orb each turn.
