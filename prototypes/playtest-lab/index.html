Moderator instructions and session script

Three overall is a pilot. The release line names a three-player pilot. The original A/B decision brief requires at least three per arm. Three players in total can show where first-time players stall. They cannot decide between the two links. Report counts, never percentages.

Before. Use a desktop browser and a new private window. Note the commit, browser and screen. Ask consent before recording voice or screen. Pick the guided or standard link for this participant and set the Assignment field to match.

Say exactly. "This is a card game. Play it for three minutes. I can't answer questions while you play. Please say what you're thinking out loud."

Run. Give no task, goal or prompt. Press Start in the timer when the page is visible. Press the two Mark buttons as each event happens. Stop at 3:00 whatever is on screen.

After the clock stops. Ask these two questions and write the answers down word for word. 1. "What are you trying to do in this game?" 2. "What happened in the last clash you saw, and why?" Put answer 1 in the goal field and answer 2 in the observations field.

The four observations.

  1. Time to the first summon, in seconds.
  2. Time to the first End turn, in seconds, and whether it came before any summon.
  3. Round reached, or the result, at 3:00.
  4. What they say the goal is, word for word.
