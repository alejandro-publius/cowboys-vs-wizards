# How Kiro changed Cowboys vs. Wizards

**Judge entry · updated November 19, 2026 Pacific · describes `main` at `a9aa828`**

Cowboys vs. Wizards is a haunted-Western card duel that runs in a browser. The fight in the December 4 entry is **Showdown**: you choose every attack, your troops shield your Base, and turns alternate. Alex chose it on November 19 after playing it. Fable 5.2 wrote its rules ([rulebook](../../prototypes/showdown/RULEBOOK.md), PR [#142](https://github.com/alejandro-publius/cowboys-vs-wizards/pull/142)). Kiro CLI moved it into the game, put all 29 cards in one catalog and added ten readable combat cues.

This page is the one judge-facing description. The [Devpost packet](devpost-packet.md), [build story](build-story.md) and [historical evidence map](../kiro-brag-sheet.md) give more detail and older history.

## Try one action (about a minute)

1. Open [graybox/?seed=1](https://alejandro-publius.github.io/cowboys-vs-wizards/graybox/?seed=1) and press **Play**. Boot Hill starts with two Sheriffs and two Deadeyes in your hand.
2. Click a **Sheriff**. The log reads "The Cowboys' Sheriff arrived." Nobody attacks on the opening turn, so press **End turn**.
3. The Wizards summon a Skeleton. On your turn, click your Sheriff on the board, then **Take cover**.
4. The "Take cover" cue flashes on your Sheriff's slot, and the log line "The Cowboys' Sheriff took cover." lights up. A troop in cover keeps damage off your Base and does not hit back.

Fact check: Kiro CLI ran these steps by mouse clicks in headless Chrome against the live page on November 19, 2026 (Pacific), with no page errors. That was a one-off fact check for this page, not a new test suite.
