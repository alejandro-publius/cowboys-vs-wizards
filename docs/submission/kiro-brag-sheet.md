Cowboys vs. Wizards is a haunted-Western card duel that runs in a browser. The fight in the December 4 entry is **Showdown**: you choose every attack, your troops shield your Base, and turns alternate. Alex chose it on November 19 after playing it. Fable 5.2 wrote its rules ([rulebook](../../prototypes/showdown/RULEBOOK.md), PR [#142](https://github.com/alejandro-publius/cowboys-vs-wizards/pull/142)). The site root opens the Showdown menu. Camp contains the Trail, rivals, Wanted hunts, packs, deck builder, best of three, two-player mode and the Sheriff’s Ledger. ## Try one action (about a minute)

1. Open [graybox/?seed=1](https://alejandro-publius.github.io/cowboys-vs-wizards/graybox/?seed=1), press **Play**, then click **Boot Hill** in the Camp. You start with two Sheriffs and two Deadeyes in your hand.
2. Click a **Sheriff**. The log reads "The Cowboys' Sheriff arrived." Nobody attacks on the opening turn, so press **End turn**.
3. The Wizards summon a Skeleton. On your turn, click your Sheriff on the board, then **Take cover**.
4. The "Take cover" cue flashes on your Sheriff's slot, and the log line "The Cowboys' Sheriff took cover." lights up. A troop in cover keeps damage off your Base and does not hit back.

Fact check: Kiro CLI ran these steps by mouse clicks in headless Chrome against the live page built from `a9aa828` on November 19, 2026 (Pacific), with no page errors. That was before the Camp existed. On `4d52642` the Kiro CLI audit session replayed the same seed-1 actions through the game's own session code in Node and got the same Sheriff, Skeleton and cover lines; the Play → Camp → Boot Hill route is covered by `browser-showdown-check.mjs` above. These are one-off fact checks for this page, not a new test suite.
