# How Kiro changed Cowboys vs. Wizards

**Judge entry · updated November 19, 2026 Pacific · describes `main` at [`6c14686`](https://github.com/alejandro-publius/cowboys-vs-wizards/commit/6c14686cfe22d948913d1ebadf366320519de509) (merge of #153)**

Cowboys vs. Wizards is a haunted-Western card duel that runs in a browser. The fight in the December 4 entry is **Showdown**: you choose every attack, your troops shield your Base, and turns alternate. Alex chose it on November 19 after playing it. Fable 5.2 wrote its rules ([rulebook](../../prototypes/showdown/RULEBOOK.md), PR [#142](https://github.com/alejandro-publius/cowboys-vs-wizards/pull/142)). Kiro CLI moved it into the game, put all 29 cards in one catalog, added ten readable combat cues, made the Scout bot fair, pointed Night Watch at Showdown and added a Showdown agent profile.

This page is the one judge-facing description. The [Devpost packet](devpost-packet.md), [build story](build-story.md) and [historical evidence map](../kiro-brag-sheet.md) give more detail and older history.

## Try one action (about a minute)

1. Open [graybox/?seed=1](https://alejandro-publius.github.io/cowboys-vs-wizards/graybox/?seed=1) and press **Play**. Boot Hill starts with two Sheriffs and two Deadeyes in your hand.
2. Click a **Sheriff**. The log reads "The Cowboys' Sheriff arrived." Nobody attacks on the opening turn, so press **End turn**.
3. The Wizards summon a Skeleton. On your turn, click your Sheriff on the board, then **Take cover**.
4. The "Take cover" cue flashes on your Sheriff's slot, and the log line "The Cowboys' Sheriff took cover." lights up. A troop in cover keeps damage off your Base and does not hit back.

Fact check: Kiro CLI ran these steps by mouse clicks in headless Chrome against the live page built from `a9aa828` on November 19, 2026 (Pacific), with no page errors. After the Scout fix merged, a Kiro IDE session replayed the same four actions through the game's own session code at `6c14686` in Node, without a browser: the same Sheriff, Skeleton and cover events came out. Both were one-off fact checks for this page, not a new test suite.
