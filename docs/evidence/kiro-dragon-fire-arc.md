# Ember Gulch and the Bonewright

Fable 5.2 designed the Dragon Fire rule, the three boards and the Rebuild response. Three Kiro CLI workers built the engine rule, encounter flow and board feedback in parallel. Codex integrated their changes, connected authored stages to the shared rules option and checked the routes in Chrome. Kiro migrated the standalone Dragon Fire prototype to that same engine rule.

## Play or record

Build with `npm run build` and open `dist/cowboys-vs-wizards-board-duel.html?mode=fire-arc`. The menu also has a Fire arc draft link.

1. At Ember Gulch, shoot the Dragon with Deadeye and end the turn. The fire passes through the one-orb Sheriff and the Dragon escapes. Replay. Tribute the Sheriff to summon Billy into lane 2, shoot the Dragon with Deadeye, then end the turn. Billy survives and the Dragon is captured.
2. Choose **Face the Bonewright**. The rival moved first on this board. Tribute the lane 2 Sheriff to summon Captured Dragon in that lane, use Deadeye on the Skeleton in lane 2, then end the turn. Three fire damage takes the Wizard Base from 3 to 0. The Cowboy wins in round 7.
3. Choose **Same board, you move first**. Repeat that combo into the marked lane. The rival replaces the wounded Skeleton with a fresh Wizard. In round 8 summon a Sheriff into lane 1 and end the turn. The loss explains what the rebuild did.
4. Replay position B. Start with Sheriff in lane 3 and end the turn. In round 8 summon a Sheriff into lane 1, use Deadeye on the Wizard in lane 2, then end the turn. In round 9 tribute the Sheriff in lane 3 and summon Captured Dragon into lane 2. End the turn to win.

The browser replay verified both victories, both failure routes, the visible lane 2 mark and no change to the local player profile. The draft is outside counted progression. The seven selected before-and-after outcomes matched across the three standalone prototype positions after migration. Human play and native Kiro footage are pending.
