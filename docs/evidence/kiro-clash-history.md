# Earlier clashes

Kiro CLI added a per-deal history in the graybox. Latest clash remains
visible. After two clashes, **Earlier clashes** opens the completed
summaries newest first. It uses the same public event summaries, with
the older rounds outside the live announcement region.

Codex verified the Night Riders win route against Stargazer on seed 4.
Chrome showed round 1 after the second clash and rounds 3, 2 and 1 after
the final clash. The disclosure stayed open through card selection and
later clashes. Same-deal replay cleared the previous history.

All 13 clash log, property, and card model tests passed.
