# Reward Brief: Mounted Billy (`cowboy-mounted-billy`)

- **Card:** `cowboy-mounted-billy` (existing Cowboy Ride troop, 2 tributes, 3 attack / 5 orbs).
- **Bounty role:** The proposed reward for clearing all five hunts of *The Bone Marshal* is a guaranteed copy of `cowboy-mounted-billy` for players who want a second copy after the Trail. The Rare deck limit is two.
- **Deck tradeoff:** Billy costs two troops. He can ride past troops for 1 Base orb once Guard is cleared, or deal his full 3 attack to an undefended Base.
- **Before a playable release:** Packs and card offers can already give a player both copies. The current hunt payout would add a third, and the save loader would reject that collection. Decide what a player at the cap receives and check a save-code round trip before adding this hunt to Camp.
